Hello 👋

I'm Dũng — an AI/ML Engineer based in Vietnam. I specialize in building production-grade AI systems: from multi-provider LLM gateways to multi-agent architectures and mobile AI deployments.

I'm driven by a belief that the best AI systems combine the creativity of large language models with the reliability of deterministic engineering. This philosophy shows up in everything I build — hybrid AI+code pipelines, resilient infrastructure with circuit breakers, and human-in-the-loop agent systems.

I communicate in Vietnamese, English, and Japanese, and have a particular interest in Southeast Asian language processing, especially Vietnamese-Japanese translation.

What I Do

I design and build systems that bring AI from prototype to production. My current focus areas include LLM orchestration and multi-agent systems, on-device AI for mobile (speech recognition, NLP), and DevOps automation using AI agents.
